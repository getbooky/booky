# Booky

Ebook automation for your shelf — watch your reading lists, grab new books the
moment they appear or release, file them into per-person libraries with clean
metadata, and read them anywhere: in the browser, over OPDS, or synced to
KoReader.

New here? The [README](https://github.com/getbooky/booky#readme) has the
quick tour and the Docker one-liner. The wiki is the deeper reference:
